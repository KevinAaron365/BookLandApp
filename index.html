Privacy Policy for Book Land

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Book Land ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Book Land Android application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

Information Collection and Use

To provide a personalized experience, Book Land utilizes:

  • Google Authentication: To allow you to sign in securely using your Google account.
  • Google Books API: To search for books and retrieve book details.
  • Firebase Firestore: To store your personal reading lists, goals, favorite books, and community discussions.

Data Security

We value your trust in providing your personal information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.
